Three taps and you're talking

  1. Press the button

    Hit Start talking. A new tab opens.

  2. Allow the mic

    Your browser asks once. Say yes.

  3. Ask away

    Speak normally and wait for the reply.

Little things that help

Wear earphones

Otherwise it hears its own voice from your speaker and gets confused.

Let it finish

Wait until it stops speaking before you ask the next thing.

Keep it short

Free minutes are limited, so a two or three minute chat is perfect.
